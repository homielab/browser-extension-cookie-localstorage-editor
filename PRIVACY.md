# Privacy policy

_Cookie & LocalStorage Editor by HomieLab. Last updated 9 October 2026._

This extension collects no personal data, and nothing it handles ever leaves
your computer.

## What it accesses

When you open the popup on a website you have allowed, the extension reads that
site's cookies and local storage so it can show them to you, and changes or
deletes them when you ask it to. It does this only for the site in the tab you
opened it on, and only after you have granted access to that site (or to all
sites) through your browser's own permission prompt.

## What it stores

The extension keeps one setting in its own storage on your computer: which tab
of the popup, Cookies or Local storage, you used last. It keeps no copy of any
cookie or stored value; exports are only written where you choose to paste or
save them.

## What it shares

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of any kind: no analytics, no
telemetry, no crash reports, no remote code. Cookie and storage values are
never sold, transferred or used for any purpose other than showing and editing
them at your request.

The heart link in the popup opens ko-fi.com in a new tab only when you click it;
the extension sends nothing there.

## Your control

You can revoke the extension's access to any site at any time from your
browser's extensions menu, and removing the extension removes its setting.
